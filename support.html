Support

Sheaf is made by one person. Questions and bug reports reach me directly, and I answer them.

Getting help

The fastest route is to open an issue on GitHub. Describe what you did, what happened, and what you expected instead. If the app misbehaved with a particular document, say what kind of document it was — that is usually the detail that solves it.

Common questions

Where does my library live?
In Sheaf's own iCloud container, so every Mac signed into the same Apple Account opens the same library. If iCloud Drive is switched off, Sheaf keeps the library locally instead and tells you so.
What happens to the files I import?
Sheaf copies them into its library. Deleting the originals afterwards costs you nothing, and the app never modifies the files you imported.
Which files can I add?
PDFs and images — PNG, JPEG, HEIC and TIFF — either one at a time or a whole folder at once.
Does Sheaf need an account?
No. There is no sign-up, no login, and no server of mine involved at any point.
What does it cost?
$4.99 once, on the Mac App Store. There is no subscription and nothing further to buy inside the app.
What does it require?
macOS 14 Sonoma or later, on Apple Silicon or Intel.

Privacy

Sheaf collects nothing. The App Store version makes no network requests at all: there is no analytics, no crash reporting, and no telemetry of any kind. Your documents stay on your Mac and in your own iCloud account, which I have no access to.
