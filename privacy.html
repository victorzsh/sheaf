Privacy Policy

Sheaf collects nothing. This page says so in the detail a policy is expected to have.

What Sheaf collects

Nothing. Sheaf has no analytics, no crash reporting, no telemetry, no advertising identifiers, and no third-party SDKs. It does not ask for an account, an email address, or a name, because it has nowhere to send them.

Network activity

The App Store version of Sheaf makes no network requests of its own. The only data that leaves your Mac is what Apple's own iCloud Drive syncs on your behalf, between your devices and your own Apple Account, under Apple's privacy policy rather than mine. I have no access to that container and no way to read what is in it.

Your documents

The files you import are copied into Sheaf's library, which lives either in your iCloud container or in the application support folder on your Mac. They are never uploaded anywhere else, never inspected, and never used to train anything. Text extracted for search is stored in a local cache on the same Mac and is never synced.

Children

Sheaf is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If a future version of Sheaf ever collects anything, this page will say what and why before that version ships, and the App Store product page will show it too.

Contact

Questions about this policy can go to the issue tracker.
